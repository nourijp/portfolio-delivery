About Me

Delivery-minded communicator for SaaS rollouts, enablement, and adoption.

I’m Hamed Nouri, a delivery and customer enablement specialist with experience supporting SaaS/product rollouts, onboarding, training, documentation, stakeholder alignment, and post-launch adoption. My work sits between communication, implementation, product support, and user education.

At CiraConnect, I supported SaaS communications, training, documentation, and adoption across internal, support, and customer-facing teams. I created FAQs, guides, training content, knowledge base resources, rollout updates, and feedback loops that helped users understand platform changes and reduce adoption friction.

I’m strongest when the work involves many moving parts: technical and non-technical stakeholders, changing requirements, customer or user confusion, documentation gaps, training needs, and the need to turn messy implementation details into clear next steps.

How I Support Delivery

A proven six-step flow to handle complex rollouts and enablement efforts.

1. Discover

Understand stakeholder needs, user pain points, business goals, and rollout context.

2. Align

Clarify timelines, responsibilities, handoffs, training needs, communication plans, and success metrics.

3. Enable

Create guides, FAQs, training content, templates, announcements, and self-service resources.

4. Launch

Support rollout messaging, user readiness, stakeholder coordination, and adoption support.

5. Measure

Review usage data, feedback, support trends, survey responses, and communication engagement.

6. Improve

Refine documentation, training, workflows, and follow-up resources to reduce friction.
